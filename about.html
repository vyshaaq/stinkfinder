CAESAR-m

"Deep Learning for a Better Web."

Caesar-M (Comments Analysis and Evaluation Screening Algorithm for Rogue Detection in Malayalam) harnesses the power of advanced neural networks to promote positive online interactions. Utilizing architectures like LSTM and fine-tuned mBERT, the model excels in filtering rogue words, slang, and slurs in Malayalam YouTube comments. Trained with TensorFlow on a rich, custom Malayalam dataset, Caesar-M delivers precision and scalability. By applying state-of-the-art deep learning and natural language processing, it aims to create a cleaner, safer digital space for meaningful conversations.

Fine-tuned mBERT for Malayalam

Accurately captures sentiment in Malayalam text by leveraging the capabilities of fine-tuned multilingual BERT models.

LSTM-based Classification

Utilizes Long Short-Term Memory networks to process sequential data and effectively identify rogue comments.

Custom Malayalam Dataset

Built a specialized dataset tailored for Malayalam sentiment analysis, ensuring high relevance and precision in results.

Robust Training Pipeline

Employs a multi-phase training process with data preprocessing, model fine-tuning, and iterative validation for optimal performance.
